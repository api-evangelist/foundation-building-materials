Fire-rated walls and ceilings are built as complete systems, so the board, framing, fasteners, layers, insulation, and installation method all need to work together as part of the specified assembly. The article highlights Gold Bond Fire-Shield Gypsum Board, featuring GridMarX guide marks to assist installers, which FBM distributes through its partnership with National Gypsum.
