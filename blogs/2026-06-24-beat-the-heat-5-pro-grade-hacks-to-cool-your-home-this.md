MyFBM outlines five professional cooling strategies for homeowners: installing radiant barrier foil in attics to reflect up to 97% of radiant heat, sealing air leaks with expanding spray foam, adding rigid foam boards to reduce thermal bridging, using vapor barriers in crawlspaces to manage moisture, and upgrading to mineral wool insulation instead of standard fiberglass for better thermal performance.
